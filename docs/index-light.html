The bakers behind the magic

Heidi & Ingrid

Heidi Dawson & Ingrid Newman

Maison des Gâteaux is the creative partnership of two longtime friends, Heidi Dawson and Ingrid Newman. Together they bring a rare combination of artistry, warmth, and genuine joy to every cake they create.

Between them, they've made cakes for weddings, birthdays, baby showers, graduations, retirements, and every kind of celebration in between — from breathtakingly beautiful floral tiers to one-of-a-kind novelty cakes that leave people speechless. They do both with the same level of care and the same love for the craft.

What they love most is the moment a client sees their finished cake for the first time. That look of surprise and delight is what keeps them going — it's why every detail matters, and why no request ever feels too big.

— Heidi & Ingrid
