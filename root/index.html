The baker behind the magic

Heidi Dawson

Baker · Artist · Force of Nature

Heidi Dawson will spend forty hours hand-crafting hundreds of individual sugar roses for your wedding cake, then hand it to you while cracking a joke about it. Big heart. Big personality. Enormous talent.

She has made cakes shaped like motorcycles, guitars, football fields, skulls, snare drums, steak dinners, Xbox controllers, and a dentist's dentures. She has also made some of the most breathtakingly beautiful floral and wedding cakes you have ever seen. She does both. Equally well. Without breaking a sweat.

No idea is too wild. No cake is too complicated. And nothing makes her happier than watching someone's face when they walk in the room and see what she made for them.

— that's Heidi.
